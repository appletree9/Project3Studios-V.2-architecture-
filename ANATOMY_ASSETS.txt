ANATOMICAL MODEL ASSETS

male/female macrodetail targets, muscle-definition targets, and high-poly eyes).

Original asset notices identify the MakeHuman team, Data Collection AB,
Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier and original target author Manuel Bastioni.
Only graphical model/target data was used. MakeHuman program source code
was not copied into this app.

Preparation: adult male/female targets blended across the three bundled
ancestry variations; muscle/torso tone targets applied; helper/cage geometry
removed; Catmull-Clark subdivision; standalone heads cropped and capped at
the neck; eye proxy mapped to each adult; eye rear boundaries closed;
coordinates normalized to this editor; packed as positions and triangle indices.

Both bodies contain separated fingers and toes with authored hand/foot forms.
Heads contain modeled ears, eyelids, eye geometry, nose/nostrils, lips and jaw.
These are external-surface human anatomy meshes for sculpting. They do not
contain internal organs, an anatomical skeleton, or a rig with posed fingers.
The eye parts remain separate closed surfaces within the same editable object.

anatomy-assets.js is bundled locally and cached by the PWA. Model assets do
not require a model-hosting service at runtime. The app's existing Three.js
CDN dependency remains.
