OBJECT GRAB / TRANSFORM
TRANSFORM SNAP

Check Move, Rotate or Scale to enable snapping. Move uses grid units, Rotate uses degrees, and Scale uses percentages of the starting size.

Choose a movement plane or axis. Drag the selected object; snap rounds world coordinates to the chosen increment. Grid snap never moves locked axes.

Choose a transform tool and drag the selected object in the viewport. Move works in the camera plane; drag a colored arrow to move along one axis. Rotate turns around the camera-facing axis. Choose Scale and drag a colored box on either side of the object to stretch only that dimension, keeping the opposite side fixed. Red: X · Green: Y · Blue: Z. Use Uniform Scale below to resize all dimensions together.

Select a mesh

Subdivision increases actual polygons for finer sculpting. Use cautiously on phones. The operation is limited to 200,000 triangles.

HIGH-RESOLUTION RENDER

High and Ultra render at a larger size, then reduce to your chosen output resolution for cleaner edges. Quality is adjusted to your device limits. PNG export is lossless; this setting does not add mesh detail.
